===== MAIN MOD (changes_2.0.3-TUR.13.txt) =====
[h1]Update 2.0.3-TUR.13 · Economy Recovery Fix[/h1]
The 2001 economic crash now has a way out.

[h2]Economic crash recovery[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]"No Economic Plan" can be removed.[/b] After the 2001 crash it stayed on Turkey for the rest of the game, which capped the economic cycle at Recession. Nothing in the submod could remove it.
[*][b]New decision: Economic Crisis Recovery Plan.[/b] Open Decisions > Economic Disaster. It costs 100 political power and takes 70 days. When it finishes, "No Economic Plan" is removed and the economic cycle can be raised again. A year of Economic Insecurity follows (-5% stability).
[*][b]The Economic Disaster tab is back.[/b] The crash event said that a mission had opened in the Decisions menu, but the tab and the mission did not exist. The "Incoming Disaster" mission is restored: after the recovery plan, reach stable growth, fast growth or an economic boom within 175 days. If you miss it, the Welfare Party (FP) is banned and the AKP forms, as in Millennium Dawn.
[*]Saves that already had the crash get the tab and the mission when they load this version. The 175 days start that day.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Built for Millennium Dawn 2.0.3 and Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.
[*]Please update the language pack together with this mod.
[/list]
