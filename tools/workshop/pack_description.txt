[h2]English[/h2]
The official Turkish language pack for Millenium Dawn +Turkey. It translates the whole mod together with Millennium Dawn's interface, core mechanics, European Union and Middle East content: over 64,000 lines in total. Load it below Millenium Dawn +Turkey and keep the game language set to English.
