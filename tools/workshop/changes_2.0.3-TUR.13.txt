[h1]Update 2.0.3-TUR.13 · Economy & Government Lock Fixes[/h1]
The 2001 economic crash now has a way out, and the government can no longer change under the Atatürk tree or the military trees.

[h2]Economic crash recovery[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]"No Economic Plan" can be removed.[/b] After the 2001 crash it stayed on Turkey for the rest of the game, which capped the economic cycle at Recession. Nothing in the submod could remove it.
[*][b]New decision: Economic Crisis Recovery Plan.[/b] Open Decisions > Economic Disaster. It costs 100 political power and takes 70 days. When it finishes, "No Economic Plan" is removed and the economic cycle can be raised again. A year of Economic Insecurity follows (-5% stability).
[*][b]The Economic Disaster tab is back.[/b] The crash event said that a mission had opened in the Decisions menu, but the tab and the mission did not exist. The "Incoming Disaster" mission is restored: after the recovery plan, reach stable growth, fast growth or an economic boom within 175 days. If you miss it, the Welfare Party (FP) is banned and the AKP forms, as in Millennium Dawn.
[*]Saves that already had the crash get the tab and the mission when they load this version. The 175 days start that day.
[/list]

[h2]Atatürk path[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]An election can no longer take power away from the Atatürk path.[/b] A player who opened the path saw the Liberal Democrats win an election; the government changed but the focus tree stayed on Atatürk, and the game got stuck.
[*]Now the party that rules when Atatürk returns stays in office and elections are suspended. "Declare Elections" hands the country to Atatürk's CHP and keeps elections suspended until he takes the presidency.
[*]If anything still changes the government on this path (a coup, a Millennium Dawn event), the party that belongs to the path is put back in office within a month and a short event tells you.
[*]Saves that opened the path before this update lock the party in office when they load. If yours is stuck, "Declare Elections" continues the path.
[/list]

[h2]Military trees[/h2]
[list]
[*]The military coup tree and the Armed Forces dictatorship tree need a nationalist Turkey to go on. If a coup, an election or an event takes Turkey out of the nationalist group while one of them is open, the last nationalist party is put back in office and a short event tells you. The coup tree is free again once its "Open Elections" focus is done, so the junta can still hand over to the elections.
[/list]
