[h1]Update 2.0.3-TUR.12 · Arsenal & Icons Update[/h1]
Turkey's armed forces now carry their real names, 325 focuses get brand-new icons, and a crash in the Turkic Union branch is fixed.

[h2]Crash fix[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The game no longer crashes in the Turkic Union branch.[/b] Moving the mouse over "Launch a Coup", even while scrolling past it, crashed the game while it built the focus tooltip. This could happen in every tree that has the Turkic Union branch, including the Atatürk tree.
[/list]

[h2]Turkish names for Turkey's arsenal[/h2]
Millennium Dawn gave Turkey American names for many aircraft (F-33, F-15E, A-37, F-35B...) and generic ones for its missiles and drones. Turkish equipment now uses the names of the Turkish Armed Forces and Turkish industry.
[list]
[*][b]Fighters:[/b] F-4E 2020 Terminator, F-16C Block 30/40/50/50+, F-16C Özgür, Eurofighter Typhoon, TUSAŞ KAAN Block-10 and Block-20.
[*][b]Strike and light aircraft:[/b] F-16D Block 50+ and Özgür, Eurofighter Typhoon Tranche 3A, Cessna T-37C, TUSAŞ Hürkuş-C, Hürjet and Hürjet-C.
[*][b]Carrier and naval aviation:[/b] Bayraktar TB3, Hürjet-N, Kızılelma-N, S-2E Tracker and the CN-235 MPA maritime patrol aircraft.
[*][b]Support aircraft:[/b] C-160D Transall, KC-135R Stratotanker and the E-7T Barış Kartalı early-warning aircraft.
[*][b]Drones:[/b] Bayraktar Akıncı and Kızılelma; the STM Alpagu, STM Kargu-2, Baykar Kemankeş and Baykar K2 loitering munitions; the UKAP, Barkan and Alpar unmanned ground vehicles.
[*][b]Air defence:[/b] M42A1 Duster, ASELSAN KORKUT and GÜRZ, and a missile line from Nike Hercules and Hawk to HİSAR-A, S-400, SİPER Block-1/2/3 and Çelik Kubbe.
[*][b]Missiles:[/b] Honest John, ATACMS, J-600T Yıldırım II, Bora, Tayfun, Cenk, Yıldırımhan and the hypersonic Tayfun Block-4. Cruise and anti-ship: Harpoon, Popeye, SOM, Atmaca, Kara Atmaca and Gezgin.
[*][b]Rocket artillery and armour:[/b] Roketsan TRLG-230 and TRG-300 Kaplan, and the Otokar Arma 6x6.
[*][b]Space:[/b] the Roketsan MUFS and Şimşek launch vehicles.
[*]A few late-game designs that do not exist yet have invented Turkish names.
[/list]

[h2]325 new focus icons[/h2]
[list]
[*]325 focuses across the Turkish trees swap borrowed generic icons for new ones made for them: economy, industry and energy, education and culture, the media, health, diplomacy and NATO, security, and the Turkic Union branch.
[*]Every icon is drawn at full resolution and scaled down carefully, so it stays sharp in the focus tree.
[*]Icons that show real people are unchanged.
[/list]
