[h1]Update 2.0.3-TUR.10 · The Turkish Language Update[/h1]
This is the largest update since the Millennium Dawn 2.0 release. It brings 559 new focus icons, fixes the Turkic Union crash, lets you finally win the PKK conflict, rebalances the strongest and weakest focus rewards, and, with the language pack, puts Millennium Dawn's own interface and mechanics into Turkish.

[h2]Stability and crash fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Turkic Union crash fixed.[/b] Annexing a country that no longer existed crashed the game. Every annexation, puppet and faction effect in the focus trees, not only the Turkic Union's, now checks that the target still exists first (40 effects guarded).
[*][b]Cosmetic countries registered.[/b] AZT, ATU, NCYS, TURKIC_FLX, TURKIYE_YES and OTT_federalized now have their own colours, flags and names instead of showing blank tags.
[*][b]221 missing names restored.[/b] Our localisation override hid Millennium Dawn's own Turkey texts. Counter-terrorism decisions, mafia and PKK spirits and several flavour events showed raw code names. They are back in English and Turkish.
[*][b]Party logos fixed.[/b] ÖDP, Genç Parti and AKP pointed at icons that did not exist, and the Gelecek Party never appeared at all.
[*]Mafia focuses that only showed a tooltip now actually grant their rewards.
[*]A broken colour code in the constitutional drift tooltip is fixed.
[*]The welcome window shows the current version.
[/list]

[h2]The PKK conflict can be ended[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]"End of the PKK" works during the active insurgency[/b], and also when PKK cells abroad raise the insurgency's minimum strength.
[*]A PKK uprising no longer strips your cores in the Kurdish provinces.
[*][b]New decision, "Reintegrate Kurdistan":[/b] if the PKK breaks away, defeat it, puppet or annex it, then restore full Turkish administration and your cores.
[*]The PKK decision category now explains step by step how to end the conflict.
[/list]

[h2]Decisions[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Every DSİ dam now builds.[/b] Five dams pointed at provinces outside their own state and two shared the same province, so they built nothing. Each dam now gives its state [b]+15% construction speed, +15% building slots and +10% resource output[/b], and the decision tells you so before you take it.
[*][b]Six Arrows:[/b] the category and all of its decisions have proper names, the decisions have cooldowns, and "Enforce Laïcité" is no longer an endlessly repeatable political power loop.
[*][b]Mafia arrests cost 55 political power[/b] instead of 75.
[*]Fortification decisions show their icon again.
[/list]

[h2]Focus balance[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Diplomatic tours now reward Turkey.[/b] "Visit Medina", "Visit Muslim Countries", "To a New and Democratic Middle East!" and "Plans to Achieve Stability in the Region" used to hand political power and stability to up to 45 foreign countries. Turkey now keeps the gains, and every visited country that still exists improves its opinion of Turkey by 20.
[*][b]Mass Mobilization:[/b] 500,000 manpower instead of 1,000,000.
[*][b]The 100 Million Population Project:[/b] +50% monthly population instead of +100%.
[*][b]Anarchist Science:[/b] +15% research speed instead of +30%. Human waves are unchanged.
[*][b]The largest political power rewards are trimmed:[/b] Liberalize the Country 375 → 325, OIC Briefing 350 → 275, Sacrifices of the Ergenekon Trials and Abolish the Republican Reforms 300 → 250.
[*][b]A stronger start for four weaker paths:[/b]
[list]
[*]Ülkücü: "Bozkurtlar" gives +100 political power and +5% stability.
[*]Genç Parti: "Cem Uzan Is Victorious" gives 100 political power and +8% stability (was 25 and +5%).
[*]Mafia: "Proceeds of the Rahşan Amnesty" gives 100 political power and +3% stability (was 35 and −2%).
[*]Coup: "New Turkish Regime" gives +100 political power and +5% stability (was −2%).
[/list]
[*][b]Atatürk's Legacy Fulfilled:[/b] +1.5 political power a day (was +0.75).
[/list]

[h2]Art[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]559 new focus icons[/b] across every political path. Each one is resampled straight from its high-resolution master with a gamma-correct downscale and light sharpening, so it stays sharp at the game's 100×88 size.
[*]Real people and authentic logos keep their original icons. Deniz Baykal, Ali Babacan, the CHP's six arrows and the SSK logo were not replaced with generated art.
[*]New Istanbul and Anıtkabir backgrounds.
[*][b]15 Turkish insignia[/b] for divisions, fleets and task forces.
[/list]

[h2]Turkish language pack[/h2]
The optional language pack is updated at the same time and now translates far more than this mod:
[list]
[*][b]Interface and core mechanics:[/b] the top bar, economy and politics views, money, energy, investments, influence, currency, laws, modifiers and decision categories.
[*][b]General content:[/b] country names, bookmarks, leader descriptions, game rules, loading tips, ideas and traits, international organisations, cyber warfare, missiles, doctrines, defence companies, internal factions, peace deals, operations and achievements.
[*][b]New in this release:[/b] the European Union (membership, EU laws, the European Parliament and its groups) and the Middle East, including the Gulf states' secular and religious choices.
[*]More than 42,000 new lines in this release, over 64,000 in total.
[*]The loading screen's font has no Turkish letters, so loading texts are now written without them instead of showing question marks.
[/list]
