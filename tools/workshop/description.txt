[h1]Millenium Dawn +Turkey · MD 2.0.3 Update[/h1]
[b]The most detailed Turkey ever made for Millennium Dawn.[/b] Begin on 1 January 2000 with Bülent Ecevit's fragile coalition and the economy on the edge of collapse, then lead the Republic through coalitions, crises, coups, elections and wars. Nineteen distinct political paths take Turkey from the AKP and the CHP to an Atatürk restoration, a Turkic Union, a restored Ottoman Empire and far beyond.

This submod replaces Millennium Dawn's own Turkey focus tree. It is built for Millennium Dawn 2.0.3 and Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
This mod exists to make Turkey far more detailed than the base game of Millennium Dawn. It is made by one person, so you may find historical or political errors. Reports in the comments or on Discord are always welcome.

[b]Don't take the political situations seriously and don't take this mod to RP mods![/b]
