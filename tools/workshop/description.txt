[h2]Good to know[/h2]
This mod exists to make Turkey far more detailed than the base game of Millennium Dawn. It is made by one person, so you may find historical or political errors. Reports in the comments are welcome.

[b]Don't take the political situations seriously and don't take this mod to RP mods![/b]
