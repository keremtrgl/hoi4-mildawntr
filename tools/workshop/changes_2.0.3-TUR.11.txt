[h1]Update 2.0.3-TUR.11 · History Update[/h1]
This update puts Turkey's recent history back on its real dates. The 2023 election is now decided by how you play, 24 historical events from 2002 to 2025 are new, the Atatürk path can finally be reached and played safely, and the Path Guide now walks you through every path step by step.

[h2]The 2023 election is decided by the game[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]No more picking the winner.[/b] The first round and the runoff are worked out from the alliances' current party support, blended with the real 2023 result and nudged by stability. A stable country helps the incumbent by up to 3 points.
[*]The four victory focuses unlock only for the candidate who actually won. If the election never takes place, they open after 1 July 2023 as before.
[*][b]New event: the ATA Alliance names Sinan Oğan[/b] as its candidate (March 2023).
[*][b]Muharrem İnce can stay in the race[/b] if Memleket's support is above 6%. Otherwise he withdraws, as he did on 11 May.
[*][b]Oğan's bargain is a real choice[/b] after the first round: meet his terms or let him bargain with someone else. His endorsement and his voters follow your decision.
[*]Ümit Özdağ's protocol with Kılıçdaroğlu is remembered and moves part of the nationalist vote in the runoff.
[*]Oğan's and Özdağ's endorsements and İnce's neutrality now appear only if the election goes to a second round.
[/list]

[h2]After the election[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Özgür Özel[/b] is now the fourth outcome of the CHP's 2023 congress, with his own focus and icon. He leads on to the CHP rallies branch.
[*]The CHP's 38th congress moves to 4–5 November 2023, and it no longer happens if Kılıçdaroğlu won the presidency.
[*][b]New focus, "A Seat for Oğan?" (Erdoğan):[/b] keep the ministries for your own people, as happened, or give Oğan a cabinet post.
[*][b]New focus, "Özdağ at the Interior Ministry" (Kılıçdaroğlu):[/b] the Özdağ Protocol comes due. A new national spirit, refugee returns begin, and the Victory Party gains support at the CHP's expense.
[*][b]New focus, "Cracks in the Table" (Kılıçdaroğlu):[/b] six parties won together, but there is only one cabinet. Share the ministries fairly or keep the key ones for the CHP.
[/list]

[h2]24 new historical events[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]November 2002:[/b] the AKP wins alone with 363 seats.
[*][b]The 2013–2015 peace process:[/b] Öcalan's Newroz letter, the Dolmabahçe Consensus and the collapse of the process in July 2015. They lower and then raise PKK presence.
[*][b]The two 2015 elections:[/b] the AKP loses its majority in June and wins it back in November.
[*][b]Russian sanctions after the Su-24 crisis[/b], lifted after the letter to Putin in June 2016.
[*][b]Military operations:[/b] Euphrates Shield (2016), Peace Spring and the Pence agreement (2019), Claw-Lock and Claw-Sword (2022).
[*][b]Pastor Brunson[/b] is released in October 2018. If the United States sanctioned Turkey over him, the August 2018 lira crash hits harder, and the release lifts the sanctions.
[*][b]A Terror-Free Turkey (2024–2025):[/b] Bahçeli's call, Öcalan's call to lay down arms, the PKK's dissolution, the burning of weapons in Sulaymaniyah and the parliamentary commission.
[*][b]The fall of Assad[/b] in December 2024 and Turkey's new influence in Damascus.
[*][b]Also new:[/b] the Black Sea Grain Initiative (July 2022) and the trade halt with Israel (May 2024).
[/list]

[h2]Historical dates fixed[/h2]
[list]
[*]The Russian jet crisis fired in June 2012. It now happens on 24 November 2015, when the Su-24 was shot down.
[*]The 2010 and 2017 constitutional referendums now give their results on the real dates (12 September 2010 and 16 April 2017) instead of right after the vote in parliament.
[*]The swearing-in under the new presidential system moves from April 2017 to July 2018 and needs the 2017 referendum to have passed.
[*]The Dutch campaign crisis of March 2017 needs the referendum to be on the way.
[/list]

[h2]The Atatürk path[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]It can be reached.[/b] "Strange Sounds From Atatürk's Mausoleum" needed fewer than 25 factories, but Turkey starts with about 66. It now needs fewer than 40.
[*][b]No more coups against it.[/b] Military takeover events no longer fire once the Atatürk tree is loaded. Before, an election could hand power to the AKP, the army overthrew it, and the player was moved to Azerbaijan.
[*][b]A stronger opening:[/b] the branch up to "A New Control" takes 35 days per focus. "The Father Has Returned" grants 40 civilian and 30 military factories, +15% stability and 250 political power, and the "Return of the Father" spirit is stronger.
[*]If the mission to cure Ecevit times out, Turkey loses 150 political power and 10% stability and Ecevit retires. The player is no longer moved to Azerbaijan.
[/list]

[*][b]New decision to reopen the welcome window[/b] at any time.
[/list]
