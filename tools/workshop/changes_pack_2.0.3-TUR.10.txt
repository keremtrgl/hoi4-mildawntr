[h1]Update 2.0.3-TUR.10 · Millennium Dawn in Turkish[/h1]
Matches Millenium Dawn +Turkey 2.0.3-TUR.10. Please update both together.

The language pack now translates far more than +Turkey itself. Millennium Dawn's own interface, core mechanics and most of its general content are in Turkish too: more than 42,000 new lines in this release, over 64,000 in total.

[h2]Interface and core mechanics[/h2]
[list]
[*]The top bar and the economy and politics views.
[*]Money, debt, energy, investments, influence and currency.
[*]Laws, ideologies, modifiers, the economic cycle and every decision category.
[/list]

[h2]General content[/h2]
[list]
[*]Country names, bookmarks, leader descriptions and game rules.
[*]Ideas and traits, international organisations, cyber warfare, missiles and doctrines.
[*]Defence companies and military-industrial organisations, internal factions, cartel and War on Terror content.
[*]Peace deals, operations, achievements, medals and loading tips.
[/list]

[h2]New: the European Union and the Middle East[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]European Union:[/b] the membership process and its decisions, EU laws and reforms, the Council, the European Parliament, its political groups and their MEPs, and the EU news events.
[*][b]Middle East:[/b] regional decisions and events, including the Gulf states' secular and religious society choices.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]The loading screen's font has no ı, ş or ğ, so loading texts showed question marks. Loading messages and loading tips are now written without Turkish letters and display cleanly.
[*]The welcome window shows version 2.0.3-TUR.10.
[*]New Workshop thumbnail and gallery images.
[/list]
