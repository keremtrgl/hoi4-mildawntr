[h1]Update 2.0.3-TUR.9 · Names, Pacing and Fixes[/h1]

Every party now has a real name, the trees keep pace with history, and 20 focuses that only showed a tooltip now do what they promise.

[h2]Focus pacing[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]21-day focuses now take 30 days and 45-day focuses take 50.[/b] The trees ran ahead of history: players reached the 2001 focus before the AKP founding events of 16 August 2001 had fired.
[*][b]105-day focuses in the special trees now take 65 days[/b] (44 focuses).
[/list]

[h2]Politics[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Every party has a name.[/b] Parties that showed generic labels like "Greens", "Salafi Jihadism" or "Hardline Shiite Revolutionary" now use Turkish names with their own descriptions:
[list]
[*]Liberal Demokrat Parti (LDP)
[*]Özgürlük ve Dayanışma Partisi (ÖDP)
[*]Turkish Hezbollah (Türk Hizbullahı)
[*]Book and Sunnah community (Kitap ve Sünnet Cemaati)
[*]İBDA-C
[*]Yeşiller (Greens)
[*]Sosyaldemokrat Halkçı Parti (SHP)
[*]Yenilikçiler, the reformist wing of the Virtue Party, until the AKP is founded
[/list]
[*][b]Closer presidential races.[/b] They start at 45/55 in 2000, 58/42 in 2007 and 52/48 in 2014, instead of 38/62, 80/20 and 55/45, so your choice no longer costs a fortune in political power.
[*]The candidate tooltips showed raw code text. They now say who each candidate is and what happens if they are elected.
[/list]

[h2]A stronger Atatürk's Legacy[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Atatürk's Legacy Preserved:[/b] +10% stability and +0.50 political power a day (was +0.05).
[*][b]Atatürk's Legacy Fulfilled:[/b] +0.75 political power a day (was +0.10).
[*]Eight national spirits that showed their code name now have a proper name and description, including the three Atatürk's Legacy spirits, Etatism, Kemalist Neutrality and Sectarian Strife.
[/list]

[h2]Focuses that did nothing now work[/h2]
20 focuses only showed a tooltip and gave nothing.
[list]
[*][b]Mafia heists:[/b] the bank and data missions unlock a new "Mafia Heists" decision category. Raiding the Federal Reserve, the ECB, the Banque de France, the People's Bank of China or the Bank of England adds 10 to 20 billion to the treasury. Stealing Pentagon, Russian, European Army or F-35 files gives intelligence and a research bonus.
[*][b]The Russian chemical tests mission[/b] required the USA to exist instead of Russia. Fixed.
[*][b]Anarchist path:[/b] the states you control become cores, you gain war goals against the Islamic countries of the Middle East and North Africa outside your alliance, and communism grows in your neighbours.
[*][b]DEM, "Economic Sickness":[/b] +10% stability.
[*][b]DSP, "Act Against the CHP":[/b] +3% support for the DSP and −3% for the CHP.
[*][b]Turkish nationalist path:[/b] "Act Slowly" gives one year of Covert Balkan Plans (less world tension, faster war goals, harder to spot spies). "Direct Act" improves the opinion of your allies. "OIC Compatibility of Muslim Europe" brings Bosnia and Albania into your alliance.
[/list]

[h2]Turkish names in the army[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tank hulls are named after real Turkish tanks, from the M48A5T1 Patton to the Altay T4.
[*]Future attack and transport helicopters have Turkish names.
[*]Satellites are Türksat (communications), BİLSAT, Göktürk and İMECE (reconnaissance) and UKZS Block I–VI (navigation).
[/list]

[h2]Other changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Turkish scientists have their own portraits:[/b] 26 men and 13 women. Before, they came from a small pool of 8 faces that included a masked militant.
[*]The mafia option "Allocate all the resources you can" costs 1.5 billion instead of 3.5 billion.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Existing saves load, but a new game is recommended for the focus durations and the party names.
[*]Built for Millennium Dawn 2.0.3 and Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.
[*]The Turkish language pack is updated to 2.0.3-TUR.9 at the same time; please update both.
[/list]

[h1]Türkçe[/h1]

Artık her partinin gerçek bir adı var, ağaçlar tarihle aynı hızda ilerliyor ve yalnızca ipucu gösteren 20 odak vaat ettiğini yapıyor.

[h2]Odak süreleri[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]21 günlük odaklar artık 30, 45 günlükler 50 gün sürüyor.[/b] Ağaçlar tarihin önüne geçiyordu: oyuncular 2001 odağına AKP'nin 16 Ağustos 2001'deki kuruluş olayları gelmeden ulaşıyordu.
[*][b]Özel ağaçlardaki 105 günlük odaklar artık 65 gün sürüyor[/b] (44 odak).
[/list]

[h2]Siyaset[/h2]

[list]
[*]Liberal Demokrat Parti (LDP)
[*]Özgürlük ve Dayanışma Partisi (ÖDP)
[*]Türk Hizbullahı
[*]Kitap ve Sünnet Cemaati
[*]İBDA-C
[*]Yeşiller
[*]Sosyaldemokrat Halkçı Parti (SHP)
[*]AKP kurulana kadar Fazilet Partisi'nin reformcu kanadı Yenilikçiler
[/list]
[*][b]Daha başa baş cumhurbaşkanlığı seçimleri.[/b] 38/62, 80/20 ve 55/45 yerine 2000'de 45/55, 2007'de 58/42, 2014'te 52/48 başlıyor; seçiminiz artık çok fazla siyasi güce mal olmuyor.
[*]Aday ipuçları ham kod metni gösteriyordu. Artık her adayın kim olduğunu ve seçilirse ne olacağını yazıyor.
[/list]

[h2]Daha güçlü Atatürk'ün Mirası[/h2]
[list]

[*][b]Atatürk'ün Mirası Tamamlandı:[/b] günlük +0.75 siyasi güç (önceden +0.10).

Pentagon, Rus, Avrupa Ordusu ya da F-35 dosyalarını çalmak istihbarat ve araştırma bonusu veriyor.

[*][b]DEM, "Ekonomik Hastalık":[/b] +%10 istikrar.
[*][b]DSP, "CHP'ye Karşı Harekete Geç":[/b] DSP'ye +%3, CHP'ye −%3 destek.

"Doğrudan Hareket Et" müttefiklerinizin görüşünü artırıyor. "Müslüman Avrupa'nın İİT ile Uyumu" Bosna ve Arnavutluk'u ittifakınıza katıyor.
